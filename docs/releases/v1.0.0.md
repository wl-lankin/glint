The first release of **Glint**, a fast, beautiful image viewer for Windows and macOS.

### Highlights

- **Every image.** PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, SVG, BMP, ICO, TIFF, PSD, JPEG XL, TGA, QOI, DDS, HDR, EXR, Netpbm and HEIC (macOS). Files are recognized by their content, not just the extension.
- **Make it the default.** Glint registers for all of these types, so an SVG opens in Glint instead of the browser.
- **Slideshow from a selection.** Select several images in Explorer or Finder and open them together: Glint gathers them into one slideshow with Ken Burns motion, a crossfade and a filmstrip.
- **Ambient light.** Every image sits in a soft glow of its own colors.
- **Look closer.** Info panel with histogram and camera data, a six-color palette (click to copy), a pixel loupe with the hex value under the cursor, and a contact sheet of the whole set.
- **Transparency done right.** Ambient, checkerboard, black or white backdrop.
- **Updates itself.** New releases download in the background and install when you close Glint, or right away with "Restart to update".
- Zoom at the cursor, pan, rotate, mirror, fullscreen, copy to clipboard, drag and drop, dark and light theme.

The builds are not code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may ask once: choose **More info > Run anyway**. On macOS, open the app with right-click > **Open** the first time, or run `xattr -cr /Applications/Glint.app`.
